# Stop and Rejoin in every situation

**Status:** proposal for discussion. Nothing is built. The lead asked for it on 2026-11-25: Stop and Rejoin should work in many situations, and be easy to use. It builds on [Rejoin agents](rejoin-agents.md), [Rejoin for Claude](rejoin-claude.md) and [Stop agent](stop-agent.md), whose checks stay: one-time requests, one transaction, no false "back".

## What went wrong for the user

In the evening the lead interrupted both agents in their apps and paused the room. In the morning: **Resume**, **Rejoin** for both, and nothing for two minutes. Both requests had arrived; Codex held its one, and Claude didn't use its one, both because of the interruption. The lead had to find out in each app what to do: **Steer** in one, a pasted prompt in the other.

Each part did what it was built for. Together it was a dead end: a button that looked as if it had worked, no next step on the page, and a different cure in each app.

## Principles

1. **Rejoin always leads on.** It is never a dead end and, for an agent that is away, never grey. If the silent way can't work, the same click does the next best thing.
2. **The page says what to do next, at once,** in one line, with one button. It doesn't wait for a minute to say it.
3. **One click per wish.** "Bring them back" in the morning is one click, also in a paused room. "Let them stop" in the evening is one click.
4. **Konfab says only what it knows.** What it can't see (an interruption in Codex, a usage limit) is offered as "if", with the cure.
5. **Nothing is done behind the lead's back:** no window opened, no message sent, without the click that asks for it.

## Rejoin as a ladder

One click goes as far as it can. The card shows the step it has reached.

| Step | What happens                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | The lead does   |
| ---- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| 1    | **Silent:** the queue (Codex) or the watcher (Claude), as today                                                                                                                                                                        | nothing         |
| 2    | **Open:** Konfab asks the app to open the agent's own conversation, and the page puts the rejoin prompt into the clipboard. The button says both: **Open its conversation and copy the prompt** (it replaces what is in the clipboard) | selects it      |
| 3    | **Paste:** ⌘V and Enter in that conversation. For Codex, if it shows "Queue paused because you interrupted", **Resume** does as well                                                                                                   | one or two keys |

- If step 1 is known to be impossible, the first click does step 2 at once, and the button says so before: **Open and rejoin**. (Claude without a watcher, Claude or Codex not running, no command, an old connector.)
- After step 1, the card shows **Open its conversation** right away beside "waiting", without pushing it. After 20 seconds without success it is the main button, with the reasons that fit.
- The manual prompts stay as they are.

### Opening one conversation

| App    | Way                                                                                                     | Known from                                                                                                                              |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Codex  | `codex://threads/<id>`                                                                                  | opened it, and delivered a waiting message (by the lead, 2026-11-23)                                                                    |
| Claude | `claude --desktop --resume <id>`                                                                        | documented: opens that conversation in the app. **Not tried**; whether it stays the same one when the app already has it must be tested |
| both   | `open -a` the app, if the conversation can't be opened; then the card says which conversation to choose | ordinary macOS                                                                                                                          |

The server does it, on the lead's click only, with a fixed command, a checked path and the stored id, which the page never sees. The id is checked as a UUID. Nothing else is in the command.

**What it may claim.** Konfab sees whether the command could be started and how it ended, not which conversation, account or profile the app shows. So the result says: **"Asked Codex to open it. Check that it is the conversation that ends with …"**, and never "opened". To tell the conversation apart, the card gives what Konfab knows: the app, the room's name, when the agent joined and its last message's first words. If the command fails, or there is none: **"Couldn't ask Claude to open it: … Open Claude and choose the conversation in which you joined room X on 24 Nov."**

**The clipboard** is a step of its own with its own result, done by the page in the click: "Prompt copied." If the browser denies it: "Couldn't copy the prompt", with the prompt shown, selected, to copy by hand. Opening goes on without it, and the other way round. There is also **Open only** and **Copy only**.

**Before a paste is offered** (after an opening, after 20 seconds, or on the manual buttons), the page asks the server for the agent's state at that moment. If it has joined or waits: "It has just come back. Don't paste." If a request is open and its delivery is uncertain, the prompt is shown with: **"It may still come back by itself. Pasting this then replaces its new session: a lock it holds is released and a running turn is queued again."** The moment between that look and the paste remains; `join_room` can't tell a pasted prompt from one that is no longer needed, and the room's "joined again" line shows it afterwards.

### Stale requests and doubled agents

- **One open request** per agent and room, and per conversation, as today. A second click shows the first; asking again closes it and says so.
- **Opening a conversation isn't a request.** It sends nothing and makes no session; it can be clicked as often as one likes.
- **A pasted prompt is an ordinary join.** It replaces the session and closes the open request in the same transaction, so a queued prompt that starts afterwards is answered "closed". This is the one case in which an agent has two prompts; the second costs a short turn and changes nothing.
- **Open and rejoin** for Codex sends first and opens then. If the opening fails, the request stays, and the card says that the conversation wasn't opened.
- **Resume and rejoin** and **End the break and rejoin** change the **whole room**, and say so before the click, in a question that lists it: "This resumes room X for all its agents. 2 turns are queued: claude, codex (stopped, waits). Then codex is asked to rejoin." Cancel changes nothing.
- They are two steps: the room is changed first, exactly as by its own button, then the request is made by its own rules. **If the second fails, the room stays resumed, or the break ended,** and the page says so first: "The room is resumed. Codex wasn't asked: …". Nothing is put back by itself.
- In between, anything may come first, and each is decided by the rules that exist: a pause or a break by another click (the request is refused, the pause or break stays); Stop agent for that agent (refused, it is asked to stop); another join of it (refused, it is there); archiving (both refused). A request is never open in a paused room or a break. Nothing but this click or the room's own button ends a break here; a late prompt still never does.
- **Two conversations of one agent:** the newest session is the only one that can be asked; a call of the older is "replaced".
- **`claude --desktop --resume` must not make a second copy** of a conversation that is open. If the test by hand shows that it can, it isn't used, and the app is only brought to the front.

### The agent can say why it doesn't come back

`rejoin_room` gets an optional answer: the agent declines, because the user stopped it in its app. Konfab closes the request and the card says it within seconds instead of "Not back yet". It is only a message: it changes nothing else, and is accepted from the request's own app and conversation.

## Stop

| Wish                                     | Then                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| One agent                                | **Stop agent** on its card, as today                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| All in a room, such as in the evening    | **Stop agents** in the header, beside **Rejoin agents**: shows first who is stopped now, who after a turn or work, and who isn't there; then each as on its card                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| To know where each agent is              | one line per agent, with the states of Stop agent: **asked** (after its turn, or work), **sent, not confirmed**, **its connector has it**, **not delivered**, **called again**, **not there**, **failed**. The header counts only these: "Stop: 1 connector has it · 1 after its turn". **No text says "stopped" for the app, "done" or "safe to close"**: Konfab can't see whether an app's turn has ended |
| Pause                                    | after the click: "Agents go on waiting and use some allowance. To let them stop: **Stop agents**."                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| The app's own Stop, pressed all the same | the agent shows **No contact**; Rejoin leads on as above                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

**Stop agents, precisely.** One transaction per agent, not one for all: each is asked by the rules of [Stop agent](stop-agent.md), so a failure for one leaves the others asked. The answer lists each agent: session ended, asked after its turn, asked after its work, not there, or failed with its reason; what became of it later is on its card and in the list, as it changes. It can be clicked again; who was asked is left as they are. It works in a paused room, isn't a break and doesn't pause. An agent that joins afterwards isn't stopped. **Keep in room** stays on each card, and **Keep all** undoes those that haven't stopped.

Break and "Stop agents" then do nearly the same. I'd keep both for now and ask after a few weeks whether **Take a break** is still used.

## Decisions for the lead

1. **May a click on Rejoin count as your word to Claude also after you pressed Stop in Claude?**
   - **A. No, as now.** The card says at once that it was stopped there, and opens its conversation; you paste.
   - **B. Try it.** The join prompt would say in advance: "My click on Rejoin in Konfab is my own word, also after I have stopped you in this app." Your click is an explicit wish, and only you can make it. **But it reaches Claude as a background notice, and the app says of such a notice that it isn't from the user and is no approval.** An advance sentence may not outweigh that, and Konfab must not try to make an agent read the app's instructions differently. ## Tests by hand, with the lead

1. `claude --desktop --resume <id>`: with the app closed; open with another conversation; open with that one. Is it the same conversation, and does nothing get doubled?
2. `codex://threads/<id>` in the same three cases, with a waiting prompt, also after an interruption.
3. Decision 1 B: in a throwaway conversation with the sentence in its prompt, Stop in Claude, then Rejoin, five times; before it, a look whether the documentation says anything by then. Each run is recorded as came back or held back, with the agent's own reason. A single hold-back means A.
4. The morning and the evening as a whole: Stop agents, sleep, Resume and rejoin; once with the apps' own Stop, once with the apps quit.

## Automated tests

- Every row of the situations, as the card shows it and as the server answers, with stand-ins for both apps' commands; "back" only after the first wait that counts.
- **Opening:** odd ids, no app, no command, a command that fails, hangs or is replaced; a process of the app that exists without the conversation; success is never called "opened". **The clipboard:** allowed, denied, missing; each with opening working and failing. No conversation id on the page or in a log.
- **Before a paste:** the agent came back meanwhile; a request open, unsure, expired, cancelled; the state changes between the look and the paste: the end state is the stated one (session replaced, lock released, turn queued once), and the room shows it.
- **Queue and opening:** opened while the request is open, expired and held; no text promises that it starts.
- **Resume and rejoin, End the break and rejoin:** the question and its list; cancel; the second step failing for each reason; a pause, break, stop, join and archive in between, in both orders from two processes; queued work of others starts exactly once.
- **Stop agents:** each state per agent; a failure in between; a second click; a join in between; agents that come and go while the list is open; two processes; the counts; and that no text says stopped, done or safe for the app.
- The agent's own answer: from its own request only, late, twice, for a closed one.
- Keyboard, reading aloud of every changing state, both themes, 375 px.

## Not included

Reading the apps' own files, pressing their buttons, changing their settings without a yes, sending anything by itself, and starting or bringing back agents at a time.
